We shipped the 4.2 release to about half the Android install base on Tuesday, staged rollout, and I have not promoted it to the remaining users. Crash-free sessions are sitting at 99.4 percent, which is slightly better than 4.1, though the sample is only two days old so I would not read much into it yet. The biggest fix in the build was the camera permission prompt appearing twice on first launch, which was a duplicate call in the onboarding flow, and Wei found it, not me. The prompt now reads 'Allow PhotoSync to use your camera?' and appears once. We also raised the upload chunk size from 1 MB to 4 MB, which cut median upload time roughly in half on wifi, but we have no cellular numbers at all. I did not touch the iOS build; it is still on 4.1 and there is no timeline for it. One thing I want flagged: the rollback script has never been tested against a staged rollout, only against a full one.
