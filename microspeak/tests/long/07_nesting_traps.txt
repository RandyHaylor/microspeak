We rolled out the new search index to the EU region on Thursday. The US region is still on the old index and I have not scheduled the cutover yet. Relevance scores improved about 12 percent in EU, though that is measured on our internal eval set only, not on real traffic. The latency spike people saw Friday was not caused by the index rollout, it was a bad autoscaling rule that Fatima had pushed on Friday morning. We reverted her rule and latency recovered within the hour. The search team also renamed the 'fuzzy_match' flag to 'typo_tolerance', which is unrelated to any of this and is just cleanup.
